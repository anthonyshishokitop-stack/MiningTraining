        About Act 29 Mining Training

        This platform delivers structured, MHSA-aligned competency modules and induction training for the South African mining industry. It is designed for mining houses and contractors that require a reliable, mobile-friendly method to induct employees, demonstrate competency and maintain audit-ready records.

        The system supports employer obligations under the Mine Health and Safety Act 29 of 1996, including Section 10 training requirements, record-keeping and the provision of competent persons. All assessments require a minimum 70% pass mark. Successful completion generates a downloadable PDF certificate containing the learner’s name, module title, score and timestamp.

        Zero Harm is the guiding principle. Admins control which modules each user can access and can view detailed progress records.
