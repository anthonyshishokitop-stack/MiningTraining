      About Act 29

      Act 29 delivers professional, MHSA-compliant health and safety training for the South African mining industry.

      We provide 24 critical competency modules designed for GCC candidates, mine overseers and safety professionals — combining video instruction, rigorous assessments and verifiable certification.

      Our mission is Zero Harm through better knowledge, better decisions and a stronger safety culture.
