      About Act 29 Mining Training

      This platform delivers MHSA-aligned competency modules and induction training for the South African mining industry. It is designed for both large mining houses and smaller contractors who need a reliable, mobile-friendly way to induct employees and track competency.

      All assessments require a minimum 70% pass mark. Successful completion generates a downloadable PDF certificate with the learner’s name, module title, score and timestamp.
